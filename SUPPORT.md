# Support | 使用与反馈

PairRename is a community project. To help keep responses useful and private, choose the channel that fits your question:

- **Bug report:** open an issue with the bug-report template. Include reproducible steps and redact personal details.
- **Feature idea:** use the feature-request template and describe the workflow and why it matters.
- **Security concern:** follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); do not post exploit details publicly before a fix is available.
- **Getting started:** follow the bilingual [README](README.md). Try unfamiliar file operations on copies and keep independent backups.

For a useful report, include the app version, Android version, storage provider, expected behavior, and what happened instead. Do not upload private photo libraries, full folder listings, credentials, or signing keys. Community response times are not guaranteed.

## 中文支持

PairRename 是社区项目。为了让反馈更容易处理，也保护你的隐私，请选择合适的入口：

- **问题反馈：**使用 Bug 模板提交可复现步骤，并删除个人信息。
- **功能建议：**使用建议模板说明你的操作场景，以及它为什么重要。
- **安全问题：**按 [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) 私下报告；修复前请勿公开利用细节。
- **入门使用：**阅读[双语 README](README.md)。不熟悉的文件操作请先在副本上尝试，并独立备份重要资料。

报告时请说明应用版本、Android 版本、存储提供方、预期行为和实际结果。请勿上传私人照片库、完整目录清单、凭据或签名密钥。社区响应时间不作保证。
