# Community Code of Conduct

PairRename is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and constructive community. Participants are expected to be considerate, assume good faith, give actionable feedback, and focus discussion on ideas and behavior rather than personal attacks.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, and deliberate publication of another person's private data are not acceptable. Do not post private photographs, credentials, signing keys, or sensitive folder contents in issues or pull requests.

If conduct needs moderator attention, report it privately through GitHub's repository contact/reporting tools. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when necessary to protect community members.
